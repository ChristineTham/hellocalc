# HP-35 — Keyboard Layout

- **Display:** Red LED, 15 positions (10-digit mantissa with sign + 2-digit exponent with sign); shows floating decimal point.
- **Prefix/shift keys:** None. (`arc` is an ordinary key used as a prefix for inverse trig.)
- **Logic / stack:** RPN, 4-level operational stack (X Y Z T). No LAST X register.
- **Slide switches:** OFF–ON power switch (top left, above keyboard).
- **Source:** HP-35 Operating Manual, keyboard illustration in front matter (callout legend "① DISPLAY … ⑧ ARITHMETIC KEYS"), PDF pages 2–3 — hp/manuals/HP-35.pdf

## Key grid (top row → bottom row, left → right)

| Row | Primary legend | Shifted function(s) | Notes |
|-----|----------------|---------------------|-------|
| 1 | x^y · log · ln · e^x · CLR | — | Black function keys; CLR is blue (clears all) |
| 2 | √x · arc · sin · cos · tan | — | Black keys; `arc` precedes sin/cos/tan for inverse trig |
| 3 | 1/x · x⇄y · R↓ · STO · RCL | — | Black keys; x⇄y = exchange X and Y, R↓ = roll down |
| 4 | ENTER↑ · CHS · EEX · CLx | — | ENTER↑ is a wide (double-width) blue key; CHS ("CH S"), EEX ("E EX"), CLx ("CL x") all blue |
| 5 | − · 7 · 8 · 9 | — | − (subtract) is blue at left; 7 8 9 are light/beige digit keys |
| 6 | + · 4 · 5 · 6 | — | + blue at left |
| 7 | × · 1 · 2 · 3 | — | × blue at left |
| 8 | ÷ · 0 · · · π | — | ÷ blue at left; "·" = decimal point; π is a beige key |

## Notes
- Physical column arrangement of the lower block: a single left-hand column of blue arithmetic operators (−, +, ×, ÷) sits beside a 3-wide numeric keypad (7 8 9 / 4 5 6 / 1 2 3 / 0 · π). ENTER↑ spans the two leftmost columns of row 4.
- Key colouring: blue = CLR, ENTER↑, CHS, EEX, CLx, −, +, ×, ÷; black = the transcendental/stack function keys (rows 1–3); light beige = digits, decimal point, and π.
- No gold/other shift prefix — every function has its own physical key. This is HP's first pocket scientific; all functions are single-press.
