# HP-18C — Keyboard Layout

- **Display:** dot-matrix LCD; shows a 3-line rolling **history stack** (line 3 is the calculator/entry line) plus a bottom **menu-label row** of 6 softkey labels and a top annunciator row (shift ▸, α, etc.)
- **Prefix/shift keys:** a single **gold/orange shift key** (blank orange key on the right panel, top-left of the STO row). Second functions are printed in **gold** above the keys. On the left panel, the gold characters above the letter keys are typed by shift while in **ALPHA mode** (α annunciator on)
- **Logic / stack:** menu-driven **algebraic** entry (operators keyed in-line on the calculator line); results accumulate in a rolling 3-line history stack rather than a classic fixed X/Y/Z/T RPN stack. Runs **RPL internally** but presents an algebraic/menu UI
- **Source:** HP-18C Business Consultant Owner's Manual — Figure 1-2 "The Keyboard" (left panel, printed p.18 / PDF p.20), right-panel callout diagram (printed p.19 / PDF p.21), "The ▪ Key" shift description (printed p.20), Table 1-1 "Alphabetic editing keys" (printed p.24 / PDF p.26) — hp/manuals/HP-18C.pdf

The HP-18C is a **clamshell** (hinged two-panel) calculator. The **LEFT** panel is the alphabetic
keyboard (used in ALPHA mode to type formulas, list/formula names, and messages). The **RIGHT**
panel carries the display, the 6 menu softkeys, the store/recall/menu-navigation keys, and the
numeric + arithmetic keypad.

**Dimensions:** the control section (rows 1–3: softkeys, STO row, INPUT row) is 6 columns wide;

## Notes
- **Clamshell / two panels:** LEFT = the ALPHA keyboard (formula/name/message entry); RIGHT =
  display, 6 menu softkeys, and the numeric/arithmetic keypad. Both panels lie flat when the case
  is opened like a book.
- **Single shift:** there is only ONE prefix key — the blank **gold/orange** key on the right panel
  (STO row, leftmost). All second functions are printed in gold. Pressing it turns on the shift
  annunciator (▸); press again to cancel.
- **MAIN menu:** the bottom display row shows the six MAIN-menu labels selected by the 6 softkeys:
  **FIN · BUS · SUM · TIME · SOLVE · MATH** (confirmed on the display figures p.19/p.23 and by the
  six menu maps in Appendix E). `EXIT` steps back one menu; **shift+EXIT (MAIN)** jumps to the MAIN
  menu.
- **Clearing:** **shift+INPUT (CLEAR ALL)** clears the history stack/display; **shift+◆ (CLEAR)**
  clears the calculator (entry) line; `◆` alone backspaces.
- **Arrow keys (▲/▼):** roll the history stack and move the pointer in number/formula lists; the
  manual also uses **shift+▲ / shift+▼** to jump to the top/bottom of a list, though no gold legend
  is printed for that.
- **Left-panel reference card:** the inside of the left flap is printed with a quick-reference card
  ("To Start Over, Press ▪ then EXIT" / "To Clear Display, Press ▪ then INPUT", plus an
  application→menu-keys table). It is printed matter, not keys.
- **Uncertain legends:** the shift char above **B** (marked `° [?]`) is a tiny hollow-square glyph in
  the scan — most consistent with the degree symbol. The shift char above **#** (`' [?]`) is a high
  tick that reads as apostrophe/prime but is close to a comma at scan resolution. All letter keys and
  right-panel legends are otherwise legible.
