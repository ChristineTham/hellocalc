# HP-11C — Keyboard Layout

- **Display:** LCD, 10-digit 7-segment (mantissa + sign, plus 2-digit exponent for scientific notation)
- **Prefix/shift keys:** f = gold (above key), g = blue (lower key face)
- **Logic / stack:** RPN 4-level (X Y Z T) + LAST X
- **Source:** HP-11C Owner's Handbook and Problem Solving Guide (June 1985), page 8 (keyboard diagram); primary/alternate-function description pages 20–21 — hp/manuals/HP-11C.pdf

## Notes
- **CLEAR bracket:** the four gold legends Σ (over GSB), PRGM (over R↓), REG (over x⇄y), and PREFIX (over ←) are grouped under a "CLEAR" label — invoked as f + the key.
- **ENTER** is a double-height key occupying rows 3–4 in the 7th column; its f-function is RAN# and g-function is LSTx (LAST X).
- **ON** sits lower than the surrounding keys to prevent accidental presses.
- **f** (gold) and **g** (blue) are the two prefix keys on the bottom row; pressing them lights the `f`/`g` annunciator in the display.
- Layout is 4 rows × 10 keys = 40 keys.
- All legends were legible; none marked [?].
