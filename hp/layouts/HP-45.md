# HP-45 — Keyboard Layout

- **Display:** Red LED, 15 positions (10-digit mantissa with sign + 2-digit exponent with sign); user-selectable FIX/SCI display and rounding.
- **Prefix/shift keys:** One gold shift key (unlabeled gold square, top-right of the function block). Pressing it selects the gold legend printed above each key.
- **Logic / stack:** RPN, 4-level operational stack (X Y Z T) + LAST x register. 9 storage registers (R1–R9).
- **Slide switches:** OFF–ON power switch (above keyboard). (DEG/RAD/GRD are gold-shifted key functions, not a slide switch.)
- **Source:** HP-45 Owner's Handbook (November 1974), "Figure 1. Keyboard Layout", page 10 — PDF page 12 — hp/manuals/HP-45.pdf

## Key grid (top row → bottom row, left → right)
Gold legend = function reached via the gold shift key.

| Row | Primary legend | Shifted function(s) (gold) | Notes |
|-----|----------------|---------------------|-------|
| 1 | 1/x · ln · e^x · FIX · [gold shift key] | 1/x→y^x · ln→log · e^x→10^x · FIX→SCI | 5th key is the blank gold prefix key (no primary function) |
| 2 | x² · →P · SIN · COS · TAN | x²→√x · →P→→R · SIN→SIN⁻¹ · COS→COS⁻¹ · TAN→TAN⁻¹ | →P/→R = rectangular↔polar conversion |
| 3 | x⇄y · R↓ · STO · RCL · % | x⇄y→n! · R↓→x̄,s · STO→→D.MS · RCL→D.MS→ · %→Δ% | x̄,s = mean & std dev |
| 4 | ENTER↑ · CHS · EEX · CLx | ENTER↑→DEG · CHS→RAD · EEX→GRD · CLx→CLEAR | ENTER↑ is wide (spans two columns); CLEAR clears Σ registers |
| 5 | − · 7 · 8 · 9 | 7→cm/in · 8→kg/lb · 9→ltr/gal | − (subtract) at left; digit keys carry gold metric-conversion constants |
| 6 | + · 4 · 5 · 6 | — | + at left |
| 7 | × · 1 · 2 · 3 | — | × at left |
| 8 | ÷ · 0 · · · Σ+ | 0→LAST x · ·→π · Σ+→Σ− | ÷ at left; "·" = decimal point; Σ+/Σ− = statistics accumulate/remove |

## Notes
- Lower block layout mirrors the HP-35: left column of arithmetic operators (−, +, ×, ÷) beside a 3-wide numeric keypad; ENTER↑ spans the two leftmost columns of row 4.
- Only one shift key (gold). Its legend appears in gold ink above each key. The gold key itself bears no primary legend.
- DEG/RAD/GRD angular mode is set with gold-shift over ENTER↑/CHS/EEX — there is no angle-mode slide switch.
- Manual's own callouts group the keys as: Control Keys (x⇄y, R↓, STO, RCL, ENTER↑, CHS, EEX, CLx), Arithmetic Keys (−, +, ×, ÷), plus the Gold Shift Key.
