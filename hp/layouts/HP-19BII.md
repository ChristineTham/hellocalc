# HP-19BII — Keyboard Layout

- **Display:** multi-line dot-matrix LCD (right panel); shows up to 3 stacked display
  lines (history / calculator line — arithmetic is done on line 3), a menu-label row of six
  softkey labels directly under the glass, and a top annunciator row
- **Prefix/shift keys:** a single **shift** key (blank, colored — printed as `■` in the manual,
  located left of `STO` on the right panel). Each key's second function is printed **above** the
  key; pressing shift turns on the shift annunciator (`↑`) and the next key runs its printed
  second function. There is only one shift.
- **Logic / stack:** selectable **ALG (algebraic)** *and* **RPN** modes (chosen in the `MODES`
  menu, shift + `DISP`). In RPN the stack is the fixed 4-level `X / Y / Z / T` with LAST; the
  `=` key doubles as **ENTER** (printed below it), `)`-shift is `x≷y` (exchange X/Y) and
  `(`-shift is `R↓` (roll down). ALG example keystrokes are flagged with a `√` margin mark in
  the manual.
- **Clamshell / two panels:** LEFT panel = alphabetic / menu-selection keyboard (used to type
  names, text and Solver equations, and to reach shifted menu boxes); RIGHT panel = the display,
  six menu softkeys, and the numeric / function keypad.
- **Source:** HP-19BII (HP *Business Consultant II*) Owner's Manual — Figure 1-2 "The Keyboard"
  pp. 22–23 (PDF pp. 24–25); "The Shift (■) Key" / "The INPUT Key" p. 24 (PDF 26); cover device
  photo p. 1; menu contents from chapters 3–13 — hp/manuals/HP-19BII.pdf

## Notes

- **Both entry logics:** the 19BII is the clamshell sibling of the 17BII and supports ALG and RPN.
  Mode is set in `MODES` (shift + `DISP`). In RPN the `=` key is ENTER (label printed under it),
  and the RPN stack is the fixed 4-level X/Y/Z/T; `R↓` (shift `(`) rolls down and `x≷y`
  (shift `)`) exchanges the X and Y registers.
- **Single shift:** despite the two-shift template used for some HP models, the 19BII has ONE
  shift key (blank/colored, drawn `■` in the manual). All second functions are printed above
  their keys.
- **Menu softkeys:** the six blank keys under the display are the top of every menu; their
  meaning is the label shown on the bottom LCD line. `MORE` pages through multi-row menus,
  `EXIT` steps back one menu, and shift+`EXIT` (`MAIN`) returns to the MAIN menu.
- **Silk-screened boxes above the left keypad** (documentation, not keys):
  - **MAIN MENU:** `FIN BUS SUM TIME SOLVE TEXT` = Finance / Business / Statistics /
    Clock & Appts / Custom Menus (Solver) / Text Lists; Return to Main Menu = shift `MAIN`.
  - **CLEAR:** Display & Data = shift `CLEAR DATA`; Calculator Line = shift `CLEAR`;
    Individual Character = `←`.
  - **DISPLAY CONTRAST:** Darker = hold `ON` + `+`; Lighter = hold `ON` + `−`.
  - **OTHER:** Math Menu = shift `MATH`; Change # Decimal Places = `DISP` `FIX` #Digits `INPUT`;
    Set Language to English = `MODES` `INTL` `ENG`.
- **International characters:** the shifted (upper) legends on the alpha keys are the multilingual
  accented characters and punctuation used for text/equation entry. "Units shipped outside the
  U.S. may have a multilingual label." The accented capitals (Ä Ã Ç Ø Æ Å Á Ñ Ö Ü) and the
  punctuation set were read from the cover device photo.
- **Annunciators (top of display):** shift (`↑`), busy, `(2π)` radians, low-battery, alarm, and
  a printer/I-O icon.
- Legends read from Figure 1-2 (primary) and the high-resolution cover photo (shifted). `G` → `¡`
  is the only shifted alpha legend read at lower confidence (marked plausibly as inverted-`¡`);
  all other cells are legible and left unmarked.
